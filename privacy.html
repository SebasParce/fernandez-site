Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fernandez Carpet Services LLC ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit fernandezgc.com, submit our estimate form or send us text messages.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, phone number, ZIP code, the service you need, whether the job involves storm, tree or water damage, and any notes or photos you send.
  • Text messages: the content of messages you send to our business number and our replies.
  • Website usage: pages visited, device and browser type, and general location, collected through cookies and similar technology by Google Analytics.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request, schedule visits and prepare estimates.
  • To communicate with you by text message or email about your request and your project.
  • To understand how our website is used and improve it.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Text messaging (SMS)

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories of sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. Message frequency varies and message and data rates may apply. See our Terms for details of our SMS program.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Twilio, to send and receive text messages.
  • FormSubmit, to deliver estimate form submissions to our email.
  • Google Analytics, to measure website traffic.
  • Vercel, which hosts our website.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

Cookies and analytics

Our website uses cookies from Google Analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

Data retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide our services, keep business records and meet legal requirements. We use reasonable safeguards to protect it, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by contacting us below. Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
