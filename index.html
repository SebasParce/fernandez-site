  • ✓Inspect the damage and photograph every area

  • ✓Start work only after you approve the contract

What we don't do

  • ✕Negotiate your claim with the insurance company
  • ✕Pay, waive or discount your deductible
  • ✕Ask for payment on a roof job before your 5-day cancellation window closes

Georgia law protects homeowners on insurance-paid roof work: if your insurer denies coverage, you can cancel a roof contract within 5 days. We follow it on every job.

Yes. We inspect, photograph and give you an itemized written estimate you can send to your adjuster. You handle the claim with your insurer; we don't negotiate it for you.

We are based in Mableton and work across metro Atlanta. Send your ZIP code in the form and we confirm.

Do you do commercial jobs?

Yes. We install carpet tile and hardwood in churches, offices and other commercial spaces.

Can you replace a floor damaged by a leak?

Yes. We remove the damaged flooring, repair the subfloor when needed and install the new floor.
